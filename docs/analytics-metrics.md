# Analytics & Operational Metrics

## Metric Definitions
- Gross Revenue: Sum of completed order item subtotals minus discounts plus taxes.
- Net Margin: Gross revenue minus ingredient costs, packaging costs, and allocated operating expenses.
- Table Turnover: Average dining session duration in minutes.

## Kitchen Preparation Times
Tracks delta between `created_at` and `prepared_at` timestamps to identify peak shift bottlenecks.
